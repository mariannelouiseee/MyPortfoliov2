01 — Who I am

ABOUT ME

Hi! I’m Marianne Louise V. Medrano, a graduate of Isabela State University – Roxas Campus with a degree in Information Technology, majoring in Web and Mobile Application Development. I currently work as a QA Engineer at Dory PH Inc, where I also handle Tech Support. My journey with the company started as a Graphic Artist on-the-job trainee, and I was later hired as a Graphic Artist before moving into QA. This mix of design, testing and support has given me an eye for detail and a user-first mindset. I have hands-on experience with Playwright for automated testing, plus background knowledge in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Node.js, PHP, Python, and Kotlin. I keep improving my development skills, including building fully responsive websites, through consistent practice and self-learning. I come from a family of six and am the third child, which has taught me how to be both independent and cooperative. My experiences have shaped me into someone who is patient, responsible, and always eager to grow. I’m passionate about technology and excited to keep growing as a professional—learning from others, contributing to real-world projects, and continuously challenging myself along the way.
